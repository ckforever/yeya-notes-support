About this policy

This policy covers the local diary features of Yeya Notes 1.0, provided by kai chen. It explains how diary content, system permissions and exported files are handled. The same information about app data is available offline in the app's diary settings.

What you put on a page

Text, handwriting, moods, photos, recordings, attachments, selected contact details and places are added by you and stored in the app's storage on your device. iCloud sync is not enabled in this version. The developer does not operate a server that receives your diary. Device backups and file services depend on your system settings and choices.

Permissions and system services

Recording asks for microphone access. Transcription asks for speech recognition access and runs only when the system supports on-device recognition for the selected language; it does not fall back to online recognition. Photos and files come from system pickers or file sources you explicitly authorize. Contacts are chosen through the system contact picker. Reminders ask for notification permission. Basic writing and drawing remain available if you decline an optional permission.

Maps and sharing

Searching for a place, displaying a map or opening a location in Maps uses Apple Maps services. These actions may connect to the network and are subject to Apple's privacy terms. When you share with another app, export a PDF, save to a cloud file provider or send a support email, the content you select is passed to that service or recipient. Check the destination before continuing.

App lock and local encryption

The optional app lock uses system authentication, such as Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode where available. The developer cannot access biometric templates. Local file encryption is a separate option, with its key stored in the device Keychain. To move your diary to another device, export a complete password-protected backup. Keep the backup password safely: the developer cannot recover it.

Paired devices and extensions

The current iPad version does not sync automatically through iCloud. On supported iPhone and Apple Watch apps, paired devices may exchange record metadata and reminder settings through the system connection. Widgets, the share extension and the main app exchange relevant data within the app group. Availability of these features depends on the platforms actually released.

Keeping, deleting and backing up content

Content stays on your device until you delete it through the app or system. Recently Deleted, history versions and backups you saved elsewhere may still contain copies; manage those locations too if you want to remove them. Exported backups and files shared to other apps must be deleted separately at their destinations. Check that your backup works before uninstalling. The developer has no server copy from which to restore your diary.

Analytics, ads and support

The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. If you contact support, we receive the contact details, description and attachments you send, and use them to handle your request. Materials that are no longer needed are deleted, except where retention is required by law. You can ask for support materials to be deleted by emailing us. Please use test content and do not send private diary entries, passwords or encryption keys. System diagnostics and service data are governed by the relevant system privacy settings and policies.

Visiting this website

This website provides product information, a privacy policy and support. It has no diary-upload form and adds no advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to deliver pages and maintain security, under its own privacy policy. Website visits are separate from the diary stored locally in the app.

Contact and changes

The publisher is kai chen. Contact chenkai_mail@126.com for support or privacy questions. This policy and the App Store privacy information should be updated when features or data handling change.
