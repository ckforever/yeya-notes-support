A LITTLE HELP

Support

Start a page, keep a backup, and find help when something goes wrong.

Start a page

Create or open an entry from the diary library. On iPad, use the pen control on the circular tool to choose a brush. Tap its center to open colors, or drag the center to move the tool. The mode button switches between drawing and body text. Settings at the top right includes entry actions and a way back to the library.

Which interface languages are available?

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English. Where your system offers an app-language setting, you can choose a language there. Other interface languages are not currently available. Diary text, text in photos and attachment names are not translated automatically.

Where is my diary saved?

This version stores your diary locally. iCloud sync is not enabled. The app saves confirmed edits. If saving fails, keep the app and original data, check available device storage, then retry and confirm the result.

How do I move to another device?

Use Backup and Restore to export a complete password-protected backup, then import it on the new device. It includes text, handwriting and attachments, with a limit of 256 MB per backup. Keep both the file and its password, and test restoration with disposable content first. The password must contain at least 10 characters and cannot be recovered if forgotten.

Can I recover something I deleted?

Check Recently Deleted first. Restoring a history version creates a copy and keeps the current content. Do not uninstall, reset or clear app data to troubleshoot a saving problem. Keep external backups separately.

Why is transcription unavailable?

Offline transcription needs permission and system support for on-device recognition in the current language. If it is unavailable, you can still play the original recording. This version does not automatically switch to online transcription.

What if I declined a permission?

You can keep writing, drawing and using features that do not need it. If you later need recording, recognition or reminders, check Yeya Notes in system settings. You do not need to grant every permission for basic journaling.

What if a photo will not load?

Use the retry action shown on the page and check that the source file still exists. When reporting the problem, include how you imported it, the file type and the steps you took. A loading failure does not mean that the app is waiting for iCloud sync; sync is not enabled.

How can I report a problem?

Email chenkai_mail@126.com with your device model, system version, app version, steps, and what you expected compared with what happened. Use screenshots of test entries where possible. Do not send private diary text, device passcodes, backup passwords or encryption keys.
